Sample size caveat: n=37 cartridges. With fail_budget=5.00%, a single admitting cartridge in an accepted set this small already exceeds the budget for any accepted set under 20 cartridges - so at this sample size the calibration is, in practice, closer to a zero-tolerance test than a genuine 5% tolerance test. Treat tau as provisional until the validation split grows.

IMPORTANT caveat, not just a sample-size one: NOT ONE of the 37 accepted cartridges admitted a cell, at any observed IoU down to the sample minimum - so fail_budget never actually bound, and the returned tau is simply the LOWEST IoU this split happened to contain, not a threshold discovered by trading safety against throughput. It is a lower bound on where an unsafe boundary might sit, not evidence that IoU below it is unsafe or that IoU at or above it is safe - this split never observed a failure to locate that boundary against.
  Additionally: the largest optimistic error observed was 2418 px, against a 18.3x65.0 mm cell footprint of 3045 px^2 at this framing - 79.4% of one cell's area. Every record in this split fails on AREA ALONE; the morphological vs. areal distinction admits_a_cell exists for (Task 2's blob-vs-rim demonstration) is never exercised by this particular validation split, because no crop's error gets close enough to a cell's footprint to make shape the deciding factor. A validation set with larger errors would be a stronger test of tau, not just a larger one.
