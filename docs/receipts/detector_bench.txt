IoU >= 0.5, per class, identical to recog.evaluate.per_class_ap.
Centroid and edge error are measured over MATCHED pairs only, so
they are conditional on detection and exclude every miss.

2. Faster R-CNN, torchvision-default anchors, recog/dataset val (15 frames)
================================================================
default_anchors_best.pt at the 320 x 512 resize of FDR §5.7,
score threshold 0.05 as docs/receipts/train_eval.txt records.
The mAP@0.5 row reproduces docs/receipts/frcnn_map_default.txt
(0.8736 / 0.9053 / 0.8419) exactly and its mAP@0.75 of 0.5831
to three decimals - that is what certifies this harness rather
than the harness certifying itself. This is the detector FDR
§10.1 reports, so it is the like-for-like arm for §10.5's O2
row.

Do NOT quote this arm's precision. The 0.05 score threshold is
there to expose the whole PR curve to mean_ap, so almost every
low-confidence proposal is retained and counted as a false
positive; at the shipped 0.70 threshold the same checkpoint's
counts are much smaller. Arm 1 and arm 3 run at operating
thresholds and their precision columns are the meaningful ones.
