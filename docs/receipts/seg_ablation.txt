delta_cells - the end-to-end number, in cells. Positive = cells the ground
truth would have placed but the prediction lost (throughput cost). Negative =
cells the prediction packed where the ground truth forbids them (damage risk,
the serious direction). Measured on the segmenter's OWN validation split
(recog/dataset3d_seg/manifest.json, mm_per_px=0.6250) because delta_cells needs a ground-
truth label map, which only synthetic data carries - recog/realtest has none.
Requires the Plan A packer fix (FDR 6.3.1): on the unfixed shelf-cursor logic
this figure was dominated by the packer abandoning shelves, not by mask error.

Heuristic vs segmenter on the real photographs (recog/realtest)
------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE: recog/realtest/annotations/instances_default.json carries boxes only
(80 annotations, 2 categories) and ZERO segmentation polygons (checked
at run time, not assumed). Design spec Sec9's headline framing calls for
scoring placeable-area IoU against human polygons; spec Sec9.1 itself says why
that is not yet possible and proposes re-annotation as future work. That has
not happened. The numbers below are NOT that IoU. They are placeable area as
a fraction of the cartridge ROI - the same quantity spec Sec1.1 measured for
the heuristic baseline (mean 0.218, 7/20 zero) - which needs no ground-truth
mask at all, so it is comparable without inventing one.
