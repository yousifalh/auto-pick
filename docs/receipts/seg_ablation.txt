
Segmentation ablation: delta_cells and heuristic-vs-segmenter
==================================================================
  checkpoint    : recog/checkpoints/seg/best.pt
  config        : configs/segmentation.yaml
  device        : cuda

delta_cells - the end-to-end number, in cells. Positive = cells the ground
truth would have placed but the prediction lost (throughput cost). Negative =
cells the prediction packed where the ground truth forbids them (damage risk,
the serious direction). Measured on the segmenter's OWN validation split
(recog/dataset3d_seg/manifest.json) because delta_cells needs a ground-truth label map, which only
synthetic data carries - recog/realtest has none.
Requires the Plan A packer fix (FDR 6.3.1): on the unfixed shelf-cursor logic
this figure was dominated by the packer abandoning shelves, not by mask error.

  mm_per_px         : per frame, from each frame's own render sidecar
                      (camera.ortho_scale*1000 / width). NOT a reporting unit here:
                      it sets the erosion radius, the strip's mm size and the grid
                      stride, so it decides what the packer does.
    measured        : 126 of 126 crops over 112 frames
    distribution    : median 0.8211, range 0.4903-1.0739, mean 0.8107 mm/px
    fallback        : none configured - an uncalibrated frame raises UnknownScale rather than reverting to a constant
    note            : the generator's NOMINAL framing (0.6250 = layout.area[0]*1000 / render.res[0]) is the framing at margin=1.0, zoom=1.0 and describes NO frame in this corpus. Receipts published before 2026-08-12 packed EVERY crop at that constant. Because packing is discrete and non-monotone in scale the error does not cancel between the two sides of the difference: it moved the damage-direction count, not only the magnitude.

    median : 0.000
    range  : [-2, 4]
    positive (cells lost)   : 6 / 126
    negative (cells packed where forbidden) : 5 / 126
    zero (no difference)    : 115 / 126

Heuristic vs segmenter on the real photographs (recog/realtest)
------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE: recog/realtest/annotations/instances_default.json carries boxes only
(80 annotations, 2 categories) and ZERO segmentation polygons (checked
at run time, not assumed). Design spec Sec9's headline framing calls for
scoring placeable-area IoU against human polygons; spec Sec9.1 itself says why
that is not yet possible and proposes re-annotation as future work. That has
not happened. The numbers below are NOT that IoU. They are placeable area as
a fraction of the cartridge ROI - the same quantity spec Sec1.1 measured for
the heuristic baseline (mean 0.218, 7/20 zero) - which needs no ground-truth
mask at all, so it is comparable without inventing one.

  (spec Sec9.1: with 20 cartridges this cannot support a transfer claim).
